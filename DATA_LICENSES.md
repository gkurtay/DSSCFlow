\## DSSC16 author-generated computational data



The DSSC16 computational datasets generated for the associated scientific study, including TD-DFT transition tables, derived photon-accessibility results, factorial-effect tables, robustness summaries, Pareto-analysis outputs, and other author-generated machine-readable results, are made available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license.

\## Third-party illumination data



Illumination spectra originating from external standards, organizations, or published reference datasets are not relicensed under either the BSD 3-Clause License or CC BY 4.0 by this repository.



Processed illumination files included with DSSCFlow are provided solely to support computational reproducibility of the DSSC16 study. Rights in the underlying source data remain with their respective copyright holders or standards organizations.



This applies in particular to:



\- the ASTM G173 / AM1.5G reference solar spectrum;

\- CIE illuminant and spectral-distribution data; and

\- any other externally sourced illumination spectra identified in the associated publication or metadata.



Users wishing to reuse or redistribute the underlying third-party spectral data should consult the terms of the original data provider.



\## Derived results



Numerical results calculated by DSSCFlow from third-party illumination inputs may be reused under CC BY 4.0 where those results constitute author-generated analysis outputs and do not reproduce the underlying third-party dataset itself.
